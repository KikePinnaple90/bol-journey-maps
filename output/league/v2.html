Find a Specific League Line

🎯 Journey ID: leagueline 📦 BetOnline Sportsbook 📅 Last updated: 2026-04-14
A punter tries to find pre-match, live, and outright markets for a specific league (e.g. NFL, Premier League) in a single session
Phase 1 — Discovery
How does a punter become aware of and navigate to a specific league's betting content?
1

Land on Homepage

Recreational Punter
Homepage
Neutral to slightly impatient — they know what they want and just need to find it
Player Goal

Quickly find the league they have in mind and see available betting options

Business Goal

Minimize time-to-first-bet; surface the most popular leagues prominently

Success: User identifies a clear path to their target sport/league within 10 seconds
Pain Points
  • Homepage is promotion-heavy; league navigation not visible unless league is popular (mobile only)
  • No persistent “recently viewed leagues” shortcut
  • Live and Pre-match are separate top-level sections, creating early confusion
Opportunities
  • Direct league access on homepage without many left nav clicks
Ideas
  • Personalized league shortcuts based on bet history
  • Smart banner detecting live games in user’s preferred leagues

2

Navigate to League

Recreational Punter
Left nav / top nav (Popular leagues mobile only)
Mild frustration if list is long or same league appears multiple times
Player Goal

Get one level closer to their target league

Business Goal

Keep the funnel moving; reduce drop-off between sport and league selection

Success: User finds their target league in one single entry in the list
Pain Points
  • League list very long with no single unified entry per league
  • No indication of live vs pre-match vs outrights availability
  • No way to tell where all lines for a league are accessible
  • Sharp bettors find navigation too slow; prefer direct URLs or search
Opportunities
  • Live/pre-match/outright availability badges next to each league name
  • League Hub with all content in a single nav entry
Ideas
  • “Now Live” indicator on league rows
  • Filter chips: Pre-match only / Live only / Has Outrights

Select League ▼ branch

Recreational Punter, Sharp
Left nav item or top popular leagues (mobile)
Expectation of comprehensiveness — quickly becomes frustration when view is partial
Player Goal

See all available betting options for this league in one place

Business Goal

Route user to highest-value market (live if in progress; pre-match if not)

Success: User sees a complete, unified view of the league’s betting landscape

  • No unified league hub — Pre-match and Live route to separate views
  • Outrights in a completely separate section, hard to find
  • Users must navigate 2–3 places to see the full league picture
Opportunities

Phase 2 — Selection
Once inside a league, how does a punter find the specific market they want and place a bet?
Three paths from Step 3
4

Browse Pre-match Markets prematch

Recreational Punter, Sharp
Pre-match Sportsbook — League view
Engaged if market offering is rich; frustrated if limited or slow to load
Player Goal

Find the game and market before it starts

Business Goal

Maximize pre-match handle; encourage multi-game/parlay selection

Success: User finds target game and market within 2 clicks from the league page
Pain Points
  • Not all markets available from a single left nav entry
  • Real games duplicated in the list
  • Left nav counter doesn’t reflect real game count
  • No line comparison across multiple games on one screen
  • Live-started games disappear from pre-match without transition
Opportunities
  • Merge duplicated games into one entry
  • Unify left nav entries for same league
  • Add SGP and Odds Boosters CTAs on league view
Ideas
  • Unified league content in one entry point
  • Pre-match to live transitions

5

Browse Live Markets live

Recreational Punter, Sharp
Live Betting section — League filter
High urgency — live markets move fast; every extra step costs real betting opportunity
Player Goal

Find an in-play market on a specific league game quickly

Business Goal

Capture live handle; live bettors are high-value, time-sensitive

Success: User finds their live game within 15 seconds of deciding to bet live
Pain Points
  • No direct access from league page to live markets
  • Live Betting shows all leagues alphabetically — extensive scrolling required
  • Odds latency causes bet rejections (“line has moved”)
Opportunities
  • Direct “Live Now” link on the league hub
  • Persist league filter when switching pre-match → live
Ideas
  • Live games inside the league hub
  • Pre-match to live transition in league and event views
  • Order leagues by popularity in live section
  • Push alert when followed league goes live

Find Outrights outrights

Sharp, Recreational Punter (seasonal)
Outrights / Futures section (separate from main sportsbook)
Exploratory and patient, but frustrated by the navigation break from the main league context
Player Goal

Find the best available futures market and evaluate the odds

Business Goal

Drive futures handle; lock-in periods benefit the book

Success: User finds and evaluates at least 3 outright market options for their league

  • No cross-sell from pre-match view to relevant outrights

  • Cross-sell strip in game detail views
Ideas

  • Remove separate outright entry points, fold into league hub
  • Add soccer outrights into each soccer league
  • Better naming for outright types

7

Select Bet and Add to Betslip

Recreational Punter, Sharp
Game detail / Market view → Betslip
Excitement mixed with anxiety — they want confirmation quickly
Player Goal

Get their selection on the betslip accurately and quickly, then place the bet

Business Goal

Maximize bet placement completion rate; minimize betslip abandonment

Success: Bet placed with correct selection and stake in under 30 seconds from betslip open
Pain Points
  • Betslip slow to update when odds change
  • Parlay building across pre-match and live not supported
  • Stake entry UX on mobile is poor (keyboard obscures betslip)
Opportunities
  • Improve live betting delay
  • Push users to update betslip accept-odds preferences
  • Enable quick-stake buttons ($10 / $25 / $50 / $100)
Ideas
  • “Bet and Watch” — place bet and get live stream in one flow
  • Cross-product parlay builder spanning pre-match + live + outrights
