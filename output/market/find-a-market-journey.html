A punter wants to find and place a bet on a specific market for a specific game — from sports home through event row or FSP, using the new Aux Tag market grouping.

1

Land on Sports Home Awareness

Recreational Punter, Casual New User
Sports Home
Purposeful, neutral — they know the game they want

User arrives at BetOnline Sports Home having already decided they want to bet on a specific game. They scan the page for a path to that sport or league.

Player Goal

Find the fastest path to their target game

Business Goal

Minimise time to first navigation action

Success: User identifies the correct sport/league path within 10 seconds
Pain Points
  • Homepage is promotion-heavy; sport navigation is not above the fold on all viewports
  • No "recently viewed" league or game shortcut
  • Live and Pre-match are separate top-level sections, not immediately obvious which to enter
Opportunities
  • Recently viewed league shortcut on sports home
  • Live game ticker for sports the user has bet on before
Ideas
  • "Continue where you left off" widget showing last visited league
  • Sport quick-links above the fold
Octalysis
  • CD2: Development & Accomplishment — motivated to find the bet quickly
  • CD6: Scarcity & Impatience — game may be starting soon
Data Sources
  • Mixpanel: sports_home_viewed, first_navigation_click, session_start_source

Locate League in Left Nav Engage

Recreational Punter, Sharp
Left Nav
Mild friction if sport tree is long or league has duplicate entries

User opens the left nav and expands the sport tree to find their target league. They look for a single, unambiguous entry point to the league page.

Player Goal

Reach the league page in one click from the nav

Business Goal

Reduce drop-off between intent and league page arrival

Success: User reaches the correct league page within 2 taps of opening the nav
Pain Points
  • Same league appears under multiple product nodes (Pre-match, Live)
  • No badge showing live game count or pre-match count on league rows
  • Sharps prefer direct URL or search — tree nav feels slow
Opportunities
  • Live game count badge on each league row
  • Consolidate duplicate league entries into a single hub entry
Ideas
  • "Now Live" dot next to league names with active games
  • Pinned search bar at top of left nav
Octalysis
  • CD2: Development & Accomplishment — making progress toward the bet
  • CD6: Scarcity & Impatience — game kick-off approaching
Data Sources
  • Mixpanel: left_nav_sport_expanded, left_nav_league_clicked

Scan League Page, Identify Game Row Engage

Recreational Punter, Sharp
League Page — Event Row
Scanning — looking for their game; mildly aware of more visual weight per row

User lands on the league page and scans the event list. With market grouping shipped, each row shows labeled group bands (Main Lines, {Aux Tag} Markets, {Period}) above the price columns. Viewport now shows 7-8 events instead of ~10 on desktop.

Player Goal

Spot their target game quickly and see what market types are available

Business Goal

Surface market depth at a glance; reduce FSP entry for simple bets

Success: User locates their game within 3 seconds of the league page loading
Pain Points
  • Group bands add ~30px per event row; viewport shows ~7-8 events vs ~10 before the change
  • Mobile is hit harder — fewer events visible without scrolling
  • Band labels such as "Aces Markets" or "1st Set" are unfamiliar to casual users
Opportunities
  • Suppress the band header when only one group exists (mitigation already planned)
  • Sticky game search or filter on the league page
Ideas
  • Compact band mode toggle for power users
  • Inline game search bar at top of league page
Octalysis
  • CD7: Unpredictability & Curiosity — curious what market types are available per game
Data Sources
  • Mixpanel: league_page_viewed, event_row_scroll_depth, events-per-viewport measurement (pre vs post)

User moves from game identified to bet placed.
4

Decide: Event Row vs FSP Convert branch

Recreational Punter (event row), Sharp (FSP)
League Page Event Row / FSP entry
Recreational user taps a price directly; sharp or depth-seeker enters the FSP

User sees group bands on the event row. If their market is visible in Main Lines or a top band, they can click the price directly from the row. If they need a deeper market, they tap the event title to enter the FSP.

Player Goal

Get to the right market with the fewest taps

Business Goal

Serve quick bets from the row; funnel deeper exploration into FSP

Success: User reaches their target price within 2 taps from the game row
Pain Points
  • No clear visual distinction between "tap price to bet" and "tap event title to go to FSP"
  • Users wanting deep markets may not discover the FSP without accidentally navigating to it
  • Event row shows only top price columns per group; full market lists require FSP entry
Opportunities
  • Market count indicator on each event row ("32 markets in 5 groups")
  • First-time tooltip explaining the FSP on first entry
Ideas
  • "N markets in X groups" count on every event row
  • Onboarding tooltip: "Tap the event to explore all market groups"
Octalysis
  • CD8: Loss & Avoidance — worried they are missing a better price in a deeper group
  • CD7: Unpredictability & Curiosity — interested to see what groups exist
Data Sources
  • Mixpanel: event_row_price_clicked, fsp_opened_from_league_page, fsp_open_rate
Two paths from Step 4
4a

Price Click from Event Row event row

Recreational Punter
Event Row — Price Button
Decisive — Main Lines price is visible, no need to explore further

User spots the price they want in the Main Lines or top group band directly on the event row and taps it. This adds the selection to the betslip without entering the FSP.

Pain Points
  • Only the top price per column is visible per group band — alternative lines require FSP
  • Group band label may not immediately communicate which price is which market
Opportunities
  • Market label visible directly under each price button in the event row
Octalysis
  • CD6: Scarcity & Impatience — wants to lock in the price fast

4b

Enter FSP (Full Screen Page) FSP

Sharp, Recreational Punter
FSP entry — event title tap
Exploratory — wants to see all available groups before committing

User taps the event title to enter the FSP. The FSP opens on the Main Lines tab by default. Group tabs at the top match the bands shown on the event row.

Pain Points
  • Default FSP tab is not always the group the user wants
  • Tab bar can overflow on sports with many groups, requiring horizontal scroll
Opportunities
  • Remember last-used tab per sport or surface most-used tab first
  • Market count badge inside each tab
Octalysis
  • CD7: Unpredictability & Curiosity — wants to explore what groups and markets exist

5

Scan Group Tabs to Find Market Category FSP

Recreational Punter, Sharp
FSP Tab Bar
Oriented if group names match mental model; confused if labels are unfamiliar

Inside the FSP, group tabs match the bands on the event row (Main Lines, {Aux Tag} Markets, {Period}, {Period} {Aux Tag}). User scans tabs to find the category containing the market they want.

Player Goal

Quickly spot the tab containing their target market type

Business Goal

Maximise market discovery depth per FSP session

Success: User taps the correct tab within 2 taps of FSP opening
Pain Points
  • Aux Tag group names (e.g., "Aces Markets", "Corners Markets") are unfamiliar to casual users
  • Tab bar overflows on sports with many groups — requires horizontal scroll to see all tabs
  • No indication of how many markets are inside each tab before tapping
Opportunities
  • Market count badge per tab (e.g., "Games Markets 4")
  • Consistent naming convention aligned to how bettors describe market types
Ideas
  • Market count inside each tab chip
  • Default to Main Lines; remember last-used tab per sport
  • Tooltip on long-press showing example markets in the group
Octalysis
  • CD3: Empowerment of Creativity — building their bet strategy across groups
  • CD7: Unpredictability & Curiosity — curious what each group contains
Data Sources
  • Mixpanel: fsp_tab_viewed, fsp_tab_switched, fsp_tab_name
→
6

Find Specific Market and Price FSP

Recreational Punter, Sharp
FSP Market List (within group tab)
Engaged, evaluating odds

Inside the selected group tab, user sees a flat list of markets and prices. They scan for the specific line they want (e.g., "Games Total Over 22.5") and evaluate the price before tapping.

Player Goal

Find the exact market line and evaluate the price

Business Goal

Increase markets viewed per session; surface value to the user

Success: User identifies target market within 2 scrolls inside the group tab
Pain Points
  • Markets within a group have no consistent priority sort order
  • No price movement indicator (direction of recent line change)
  • Comparing multiple outcomes requires scrolling back and forth within the group
Opportunities
  • Sort markets within each group by popularity or default display order
  • Price movement indicator (directional arrow or colour delta) on each price button
Ideas
  • Pinned "featured" market at top of each group tab
  • Line movement arrows on price buttons
Octalysis
  • CD4: Ownership & Possession — finding and claiming their pick
  • CD2: Development & Accomplishment — feeling like a sharp bettor finding value
Data Sources
  • Mixpanel: market_viewed, market_group_scroll_depth, price_button_hovered
Both paths converge
7

Tap Price and Open Betslip

Recreational Punter, Sharp
Price Button → Betslip
Decisive — wants the selection confirmed before the line moves

User taps a price button (either from the event row or inside an FSP group tab). The selection is added to the betslip. User reviews the selection and prepares to enter a stake.

Player Goal

Get the selection onto the betslip accurately and quickly

Business Goal

Maximise betslip open rate; minimise price-click to betslip-open friction

Success: Selection appears on betslip within 1 second of price tap with correct market and odds
Pain Points
  • Betslip can be slow to reflect selection when odds are updating at high frequency
  • On mobile, betslip overlay partially obscures the FSP market list, making it hard to add a second pick
  • Parlay combining pre-match and live is not supported
Opportunities
  • Persist the FSP behind the betslip overlay so users can add more picks without closing
  • Quick-stake preset buttons on first betslip open
Ideas
  • "Add another from this game" shortcut on the betslip
  • Quick-stake presets: $10 / $25 / $50 / $100
Octalysis
  • CD6: Scarcity & Impatience — wants to lock in before the line moves
  • CD8: Loss & Avoidance — anxious about price changing before confirmation
Data Sources
  • Mixpanel: price_button_clicked, betslip_opened, selection_added_to_betslip, time_price_click_to_betslip_open
Excite
Bet is placed and confirmed quickly.
8

Confirm Stake and Place Bet Excite

Recreational Punter, Sharp
Betslip
Anticipation mixed with mild anxiety about bet acceptance

User enters a stake and taps Confirm. Bet is submitted. Success state confirms selection, odds, stake, and potential return.

Player Goal

Place the bet with correct selection and stake in under 30 seconds

Business Goal

Maximise bet completion rate; minimise rejections

Success: Bet placed and confirmed in under 30 seconds from betslip open
Pain Points
  • Bet rejection due to line movement breaks flow mid-confirmation
  • Stake input on mobile is obscured by the keyboard
  • No accept-any-odds default for users who want fast placement without interruptions
Opportunities
  • One-time prompt to set accept-odds preference (any / same-or-better / exact)
  • Improved keyboard handling for stake input on mobile
Ideas
  • One-tap "accept odds change" inline confirmation instead of full re-flow
  • Bet and Watch — stream opens automatically post-placement if available
Octalysis
  • CD7: Unpredictability & Curiosity — anticipation of the outcome
  • CD4: Ownership & Possession — the bet is now theirs
Data Sources
  • Mixpanel: bet_placed, bet_rejected, bet_acceptance_rate, time_betslip_open_to_placed
Ascend
User explores more markets after placing the first bet.
9

Explore Adjacent Groups Ascend

Recreational Punter, Sharp
FSP Tab Bar — adjacent group tabs
Curious and opportunistic after the first bet is placed

After placing a bet, user stays in the FSP and browses adjacent group tabs. The grouped tab structure makes cross-sell natural — switching from Main Lines to a period group or aux tag group requires one tap.

Player Goal

Find a second or third interesting market within the same game

Business Goal

Increase selections per session; drive parlay and SGP attachment

Success: User switches at least one FSP tab post-bet or adds a second selection
Pain Points
  • No explicit cross-sell prompt after bet is placed pointing to adjacent groups
  • SGP availability is not surfaced as a tab or CTA within the FSP
  • No "fans also bet" or recommended markets within the event
Opportunities
  • Highlight SGP as a pinned tab or persistent CTA in the FSP
  • Odds Booster badge on relevant market rows within group tabs
Ideas
  • "Build a Same Game Parlay" CTA shown after first selection
  • Odds Booster badge on boosted market rows
  • "Popular in this game" widget showing top-bet markets across all groups
Octalysis
  • CD7: Unpredictability & Curiosity — curious about other groups
  • CD3: Empowerment of Creativity — building a multi
Data Sources
  • Mixpanel: fsp_tab_switched_post_bet, sgp_cta_clicked, additional_selection_added

Business Goals — Find a Market for a Game
Area: Sportsbook / Product  |  Period: 2026-Q2  |  Owner: Player Journey / Sportsbook Product
Business GoalMeasurementOverall ScoreRecommendations
Reduce time to bet
Users arriving with clear betting intent should find a line and place a bet faster after the grouping change improves market scannability.
  • Median and p90 time from sports home to first betslip add
  • Homepage to bet placed conversion rate
  • League page to bet placed conversion rate

Confirmation: Median time to first betslip add decreases post-grouping launch vs pre-launch baseline. 		Opportunity Identified Suppress band when only one group exists. Add market count on event row so users can decide event row vs FSP faster. Quick-stake presets on betslip.
Increase market discovery depth
Grouped tabs in the FSP expose more market categories per session and should increase markets viewed before bet placement.
  • Markets viewed per session
  • FSP tab switches per FSP session
  • Group band clickthrough rate per sport

Confirmation: Higher average markets viewed per session post-launch and more FSP tab switches per visit. 		Strong Upside Add market count badge per FSP tab. Default to Main Lines then surface most-popular group second. Add SGP CTA after first selection.
Maintain offering density
Group bands add visual height per event row. Keeping events-per-viewport close to the pre-launch baseline is necessary to avoid degrading the league page browsing experience.
  • Events visible per viewport on league page (desktop and mobile)
  • League page scroll depth before game row tap
  • Bounce rate from league page

Confirmation: Events-per-viewport stays within 15% of pre-launch baseline; no increase in league page bounce rate. 		Needs Improvement Ship band suppression when only one group is present. Explore compact band mode. Monitor mobile viewport density separately from desktop.
Lift FSP engagement
The grouped tab structure should make the FSP more useful and increase the share of sessions that use it.
  • FSP open rate from event row
  • Tab switches per FSP session
  • Bet placement rate from FSP vs event row

Confirmation: FSP open rate increases and tab switches per FSP session increases post-launch. 		Opportunity Identified Add market count per tab. Remember last-used tab per sport. Surface SGP as a tab in the FSP. Add Odds Booster badge on relevant market rows.
Notes
  • Users think about markets by type (Main Lines, Corners, Sets) rather than by product silo. Grouped tabs align better with this mental model than a flat market strip.
  • The viewport density trade-off (fewer events per screen) is the most significant risk of the grouping change. Measure on both desktop and mobile separately.
  • Recommend a pre/post measurement window of 14 days each around the grouping launch date.
  • Segment all metrics by sport: tennis and soccer show the most group diversity and are likely to show the strongest signal.
