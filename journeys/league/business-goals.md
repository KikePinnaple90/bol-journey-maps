## Business Goals

| Business Goal | Measurement | Overall Score | Recommendations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Build a habit of returning to the site**<br><br>_Reduce friction for users who repeatedly bet on the same leagues and make BetOnline the fastest place to access league markets._ | • Repeat sessions to league pages<br>• Returning users who access same league multiple times per week<br>• Direct visits to league destinations<br><br>**Confirmation:** Increase in repeat visits and faster navigation to league content. | Opportunity Identified | Build unified League Hub with one destination per league. Add shortcuts for recently viewed leagues. |
| **Increase sportsbook conversion from intent to bet**<br><br>_Users arriving with clear betting intent should find a line quickly and place bets faster._ | • Homepage to bet placed conversion rate<br>• League page to bet placed conversion rate<br>• Time to first bet<br><br>**Confirmation:** More users place bets after navigating to a league and average time to bet decreases. | High Priority | Simplify navigation, reduce fragmented flows, improve access to live and futures from league pages. |
| **Grow engagement across products within the same session**<br><br>_Increase exposure to pre-match, live, parlays, SGP, and futures during one league-focused visit._ | • Cross-navigation from pre-match to live<br>• Futures click-through rate<br>• Parlay / SGP attachment rate<br><br>**Confirmation:** Higher percentage of sessions using more than one betting product. | Strong Upside | Add tabs for Pre-match / Live / Outrights. Promote SGP and Odds Boosters inside league hub. |
| **Improve confidence and reduce abandonment at bet placement**<br><br>_Users should trust odds availability and complete bets without friction._ | • Betslip open to bet placed conversion rate<br>• Bet rejection rate<br>• Mobile betslip abandonment<br><br>**Confirmation:** More completed bets and fewer failures caused by odds movement or UX issues. | Needs Improvement | Improve betslip speed, live delay handling, and mobile stake entry UX. |
